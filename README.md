## ⚡ Table Features & Pinball Rules

- **Sub-Stepped Kinetic Physics (10x)**: Continuous collision resolution prevents balls from clipping through flippers or walls at high speeds.
- **Authentic Plunger Spring Launcher**: Pull and release the right-side shooter lane plunger (`[SPACE]`, `[DOWN]`, or Drag Plunger) to blast the ball up the curved arch.
- **Flipper Kinematics & Ball Cradling**: Accurate angular stroke impulse transfer gives pinpoint shot control; holding flippers up allows smooth ball cradling.
- **Cyber Multiball (3 Balls in Play!)**: Complete the **H-A-C-K** 4-target drop bank to unleash chaos with 3 balls simultaneously and a 18-second Cyber Shield.
- **Top Rollover Skill Lanes (C-Y-B-R)**: Light up all 4 top rollover gates to unlock 2x, 3x, 5x, up to 10x score multipliers!
- **Pop Bumper Triad**: Glowing electric bumpers with shockwave pulse rings and high-voltage sound effects.
- **Active Solenoid Slingshots**: Triangular kickers above inlanes that launch balls across the table with bright flashes.
- **Animated Dot-Matrix Display (DMD)**: Retro cyber header display broadcasting live scores, multipliers, combos, and animated marquee callouts.
- **Ball Save Laser Shield**: 12-second laser gate protects your ball at launch from instant drain.
